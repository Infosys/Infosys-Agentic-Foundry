# AgentOS-V1.0.0 — Data Privacy Notice & Declaration
## Why you are seeing this

AgentOS-V1.0.0 processes business data on your behalf. To keep that processing
lawful and privacy-safe, every user must understand what they may and may not do
with Personal Data when using the platform's agents.

## What the platform does to protect data

- **Data minimization & redaction:** Structured Personal Data and secrets
  (emails, IBANs, US SSNs, Luhn-valid card numbers, phone numbers, API keys and
  tokens) are automatically redacted from captured trajectories and exports by the
  platform redaction engine.
- **Retention:** Traces and transcripts are purged on a configurable retention
  schedule, and you may request erasure of your records (right to be forgotten).
- **Accountability:** Actions are attributable to your identity and recorded in the
  audit log. Sensitive actions can require human approval (HITL).
